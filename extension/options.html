FOURDOCK · PROTOTYPE

Your workspace

Four services in your dedicated browser profile, with service shortcuts and clearly labelled notifications.

Forward service notifications through Fourdock

Keep the sender and preview, with the service name and a service icon. Turning a service off restores its ordinary browser notifications.

Keep services running

Use the sidebar's down arrow or Fourdock → Hide Workspace. Closing the Chrome window closes its tabs and can discard unsaved drafts. Cmd+Q in Fourdock quits the dedicated browser. Browser menu commands belong to Chrome.

Direct service shortcuts

Use Control + Shift + 1–4 on macOS to bring up WhatsApp, Telegram, Fastmail or Gmail from another app. Within a service, Cmd + 1–4 still switches tabs. Chrome must be running.

    If an assignment is missing or conflicts with another app, choose a different combination in Chrome's shortcuts page and set its scope to Global.

    Notifications

    Enable notifications inside each service and allow its browser prompt, then allow Google Chrome in macOS System Settings → Notifications. Fourdock labels page notifications with the service name and keeps the sender and preview. Click an alert to open its service; the site's original click handler can open the conversation.

    macOS still attributes these alerts to Chrome. Push notifications created directly inside a site's service worker keep their original appearance. Fourdock does not read chats or inboxes, and does not save or log notification previews.

    After reloading the extension, save any drafts and reload each service once to activate the relay. Working logins stay in the same profile.

    Service diagnostics

    This snapshot contains service identifiers, counts and preferences. No page titles, URLs, account names, messages, cookies or credentials.

    

    Authentication checks
    Keep your existing signed-in sessions. Shortcut and notification updates do not require a new QR or login. Use only the official service interfaces for credentials.
